How it works: Enter each HVAC unit's name, the time it needs to be fully running (Target Ready Time), how long it takes to ramp up (Ramp), and its electrical draw (Demand kW). The optimizer groups units into staggered waves so that no two high-draw units start simultaneously, minimizing the peak load on your electrical system. Changes are saved automatically per schedule.

Summary

Peak Demand is the highest simultaneous electrical load across all units at any point in the schedule. A positive Buffer means the unit finishes before its target time; negative (shown in red) means the target was too tight to satisfy fully.

Wave Assignments

Each wave is a group of units that start together. Waves are ordered by required start time and sized so that no wave's total demand exceeds the largest single unit's kW — this minimizes peak load. The bar at the bottom of each card shows relative demand compared to the highest-demand wave.

Demand Profile

The stepped chart shows total electrical demand in kW at every point across the schedule. Gaps between waves are the buffer periods. Hover over the chart for exact values at any time.
